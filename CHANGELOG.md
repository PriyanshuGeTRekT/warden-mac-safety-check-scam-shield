# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Safety Check: remote-control apps (installed, recent, open, leftover helpers), sharing, device management, FileVault, Gatekeeper, SIP, firewall, auto-login, guest account, screen lock, proxies, `/etc/hosts`, startup items, privacy permissions, administrator accounts and remote sign-ins
- Shield: a stop sign above every window when a remote-control app opens, escalating when it starts streaming the screen; Close it now, Call your helper, I use this one
- Plain-English explanations with evidence and the right System Settings pane one click away
- Copy a summary for your family
- `warden` command line: `audit` (with `--json` and exit codes), `summary`, `watch`, `tools`, `close`, `trust`, `helper`
- Open at login and Quit at the bottom of the menu
- Read-only, no network requests, no administrator password
